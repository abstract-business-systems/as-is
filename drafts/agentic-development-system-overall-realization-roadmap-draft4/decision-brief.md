## What is already accepted

- The exact draft-11 design-and-implementation envelope is accepted target direction.
- The exact Draft-3 overall realization roadmap is accepted for planning navigation.

## Why this successor exists

Two accepted corrective findings apply to Draft 3:

1. A large program roadmap should not be the primary human reading burden.
2. The roadmap must state which roles implement, plan or advise, validate, review, integrate, and evaluate.

Draft 4 preserves Draft 3 and its evidence. It adds a concise decision front door, progressive disclosure to exact roadmap sections, and an explicit operational role/model assignment summary. It does not reopen the accepted draft-11 target direction.

## Recommended operating assignment

- **Plan/design author:** authorized planning owner; current project configuration provides `medium` → `@preset/abs-medium` for component-builder work. Historical Terra used `openai/gpt-5.6-terra` through an expert contract; that is advisory evidence, not a selected assignment.
- **Implementation:** admitted lower-cost `worker` or child-scoped `component-builder`, current `medium` → `@preset/abs-medium`.
- **Deterministic evidence:** separate `evidence-validator`, current `large` → `@preset/abs-large`.
- **Semantic/result review:** independent `expert`, current `large` → `@preset/abs-large`.
- **Architecture/high-risk review:** `expert`; use `openai/gpt-5.6-sol` only when explicitly assigned for that bounded review.
- **Integration:** current receiving-owner authority until the accepted target transition is implemented and adopted.
- **Evaluation/scoring:** independent accountable owner; protected seed, rubric, validators, and scorer.
- **Alternate-family review:** none by default; historical `moonshotai/kimi-k3` reviews remain transitional provenance.

The exact task-level model IDs, provider route (current project default: `openrouter`), budgets, capabilities, workers, reviewers, evaluation owner, human holders, and evidence requirements are selected and admitted at kick-off. Model identity never grants authority. See [`Model and Review Assignment`](model-and-review-assignment.md) for the complete table.
