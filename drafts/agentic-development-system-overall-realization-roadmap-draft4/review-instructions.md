# Final Exact Review Instructions — Overall Realization Roadmap Draft 4
Purpose: Define one bounded read-only review of the exact frozen Draft-4 planning packet.

## Boundary

Review only the exact four-file Draft-4 packet identified by `review-manifest.md` and the parent’s separate freeze record.

This is advisory, read-only review. Do not edit files, create tasks, launch or delegate agents, contact external services, select runtime holders or models, commit, approve, adopt contracts, authorize implementation, benchmark, migrate, retire artifacts, or merge.

Do not substitute Draft 3, draft 11, Draft 6, the assignment companion, or the historical draft-36 bundle for the exact packet. Use those records only as named evidence.

## Review question

Does the exact Draft-4 packet preserve the accepted program direction while correcting the human cognitive-load and model/role-assignment defects without scope, sequencing, identity, or authority regression?

## Required checks

### 1. Identity and predecessor preservation

- Confirm exact four-file membership and agreement with the freeze record.
- Confirm that digests are recorded outside the manifest.
- Confirm Draft 3 and its review evidence remain preserved.
- Confirm Draft 4 is proposed rather than described as accepted.

### 2. Cognitive load and progressive disclosure

- Confirm `decision-brief.md` is a genuinely concise front door.
- Confirm the human can make the single planning decision without reading the full roadmap.
- Confirm the brief shows the current decision, recommendation, consequences, risks, authority limits, and next action.
- Confirm the brief links material details to exact roadmap sections, including model/role orientation, Draft-6 first-slice blockers, and gates.
- Confirm exact roadmap detail remains accessible.
- Confirm progressive disclosure does not hide blockers, material scope, authority changes, external effects, or residual risks.
- Confirm no user interface is invented.

### 3. Model and role clarity

Confirm the packet distinguishes:

- authorized planning/design authorship from advisory review;
- lower-cost implementation by `worker` or child-scoped `component-builder`;
- deterministic evidence validation;
- independent semantic/result review;
- architecture/high-risk review;
- receiving/integration ownership;
- independent evaluation/scoring ownership; and
- optional alternate-family review.

Confirm it separately labels:

- current configured roles and presets and the current project provider default (`openrouter`);
- historical Sol, Terra, and Kimi model observations;
- proposed first-pilot assignments;
- assignments still unselected until kick-off; and
- the rule that model identity never grants authority.

Reject any claim that Sol/Terra/Luna form a permanent target roster.

### 4. Scope and sequencing

Confirm the packet preserves:

- human-facing design/prototyping;
- explicit Human Review;
- implementation of the approved design through admitted tasks;
- setup-inclusive comparison against the current state of a pinned `master` baseline;
- separate advancement, migration, adoption, retirement, and merge decisions;
- accepted draft-11 agent and skill direction;
- current-versus-target separation;
- Draft 6 as a narrow first execution-control slice; and
- sibling parallelism as a minor clarification.

### 5. Contradictions and authority

Confirm contradictions are visible rather than silently resolved, especially:

- current parent-side integration versus accepted target child-owned integration;
- historical draft-36 roster and review gates versus accepted draft-11 direction;
- setup-inclusive broad proof versus the narrower `dummy-delegation` pilot; and
- historical named-model roles versus purpose-based target roles.

Confirm no wording grants task, launch, implementation, benchmark, migration, adoption, retirement, commit, or merge authority.

## Second Kimi review

A second Kimi review is not needed. The accepted findings require presentation repair and operational assignment clarity, not a material target-design change or a new alternate-family gate. Existing Kimi evidence remains preserved as transitional provenance.

Recommend a new alternate-family review only if the exact review discovers a substantive architecture, safety, evaluation, or authority change not represented by the accepted target direction. Do not launch such a review from these instructions.

## Required report

Return:

- **Verdict:** `ready`, `revise`, or `inconclusive`.
- **Scope and identity:** exact packet paths and freeze identity inspected.
- **Evidence:** direct observations with source references; separate inferences and unavailable evidence.
- **Supported findings:** only findings grounded in exact text or named sources.
- **Non-blocking findings:** useful corrections that do not prevent the planning decision.
- **Recommendation:** smallest safe next action.
- **Residual risk:** what remains unverified.

The verdict is evidence only and does not accept the packet. A second Kimi review is not needed unless the successor introduces substantive architecture, safety, evaluation, or authority changes.
