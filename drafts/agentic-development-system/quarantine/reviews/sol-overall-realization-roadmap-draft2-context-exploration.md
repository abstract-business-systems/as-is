# Sol Historical Context Exploration — Overall Realization Roadmap
Purpose: Preserve the bounded advisory finding that the overall roadmap needed historical-context repair before review.

## Verdict

`Revise`. The prior roadmap was directionally useful but under-contextualized. The original program was broader than the parallel-child clarification, and the accepted draft-11 agent and skill dispositions were treated too much like unresolved design work.

## Core findings

- The original objective is human-facing design, explicit human approval, implementation of the approved design, setup-inclusive benchmark against a pinned `master` baseline, and separate advancement, adoption, and merge decisions.
- Parallel sibling-building was a later minor clarification. It did not replace the broad rearchitecture.
- Executable realization plan draft 6 is a first execution-control slice, not the program realization plan.
- Draft 11 Sections 8–9 provide human-accepted target roster and skill direction through whole-envelope acceptance. The live agent and skill contracts remain current until separately migrated and adopted.
- The preserved draft-29–36 package contains useful historical roster, migration, setup, and benchmark detail, but its independent human-acceptance status is not established by the current continuation records. It must not silently supersede draft 11.
- The prior roadmap’s workstream and benchmark sequencing needed correction: pre-benchmark inventory and compatibility planning may proceed before evaluation; migration execution and retirement follow evidence and advancement.
- Pilot-scope evidence must remain distinct from program-scope realization and closure.
- Roadmap review must not silently become a permanent lifecycle gate or grant implementation authority.

## Recommended correction

Create one fresh successor roadmap with a compact decision brief, manifest, and bounded review instructions. Carry the accepted roster/skill direction as settled target direction, distinguish it from live current contracts, position draft 6 as one first-slice workstream, list historical contradictions explicitly, preserve separate gates for planning, implementation, benchmark, adoption, and merge, and provide one canonical next action.

## Authority limits

This exploration is advisory only. It does not approve a roadmap, adopt target contracts, create tasks, authorize kick-off, authorize implementation, authorize benchmark execution, authorize target adoption, retire artifacts, authorize a commit, or authorize a merge.
