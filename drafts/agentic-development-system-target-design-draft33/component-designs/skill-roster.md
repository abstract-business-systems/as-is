# Skill roster — proposed target component

## Status and authority

This is a proposed classification for `target-design-v1-draft-33`. The live `skills/*/SKILL.md` contracts remain current procedure authority. No skill is created, renamed, retired, or adopted by this document.

## Purpose and users

Provide a reader-oriented classification of reusable procedures and master compositions for human alignment and later migration planning. The package is for design reviewers, orchestrators, skill owners, and migration owners; current `SKILL.md` files remain operational authority.

## Current reference

The live contracts remain under `skills/*/SKILL.md` and their current organization is recorded by `skills/as-is.md`. The historical proposal is `drafts/composable-skills.md`; it is direction only and does not create a live contract.

## Planned responsibility and boundary

The target separates atomic reusable procedures from outcome-oriented master compositions. Reusable skills may assemble context, validate, draft, inspect, or perform another bounded procedure. Masters select and order procedures for an outcome. Neither level grants tools, role admission, task authority, delegation, approval, integration, or completion authority.

## Proposed catalog classification

The draft `drafts/composable-skills.md` contains 24 reusable headings despite claiming 25 and 12 master headings. The ledger records all 24 reusable headings, four new reusable additions, all 12 master headings, and two new master additions exactly once. Classifications are `retained compatibility contract`, `first-slice candidate`, `later extraction requiring consumer evidence`, `deferred`, or `required boundary`; none is adopted by this package.

## Relationships and authority

Masters compose reusable skills, but neither level grants tools, role admission, task authority, delegation, approval, integration, or completion authority. Agents, task records, host policy, and fixed safety constraints remain authoritative.

## Inputs, outputs, and consequential flows

A composition receives a bounded outcome, resolved scope, owner, applicable records, capabilities, and policy. It returns a proposed procedure, evidence, blocker, or handoff. A missing owner, consumer, capability, or compatible contract stops the composition.

## Migration mapping

The sole migration mapping is `../migration-ledger.md`. This component summarizes the proposed classification; it does not repeat source-to-target disposition authority.

## Acceptance and validation proposal

Every proposed extraction or composition requires a distinct purpose, owner, independent consumer, compatibility path, and focused validation. Negative validation must show that direct skill use cannot select or launch an agent, mutate task authority, bypass admitted tools, alter protected fixtures, or infer completion. Positive validation must show that an authority-bearing agent can compose only capabilities admitted by role, host, and task.

## Open decisions and dependencies

- which candidate extractions have an independent first consumer;
- final naming and whether any rename needs a compatibility alias;
- exact target record and design-link contracts;
- owners and validation fixtures for each adopted composition.
