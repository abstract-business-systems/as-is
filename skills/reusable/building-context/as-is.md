# Building Context - as-is

## Purpose
Assemble the smallest authoritative context for a bounded decision or handoff.

## Design

The skill states the decision question and stopping condition, gathers authoritative records and direct evidence, and stops once the context is sufficient without widening scope. It is a foundational sibling under the Skills catalog, frequently composed into handoffs, validation, and delegation work that needs provenance-bearing context, but it owns none of those procedures. The skill establishes fit only and grants no tools, permissions, or authority; it escalates conflicts instead of filling gaps from proximity and makes no changes to owning records.
