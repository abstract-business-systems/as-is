# Recording Backlog Items - as-is

## Purpose
Prepare bounded backlog proposals for the owning backlog procedure.

## Design

The skill writes one uniquely named proposal carrying purpose, description, owner, scope, acceptance, fully qualified dependencies, preferences, and residual notes, without selecting or claiming the work.

It is a reusable sibling under the skills catalog; downstream backlog procedures such as managing-backlogs consume the recorded item, while component-task procedures such as implementing-tasks may later pick it up through their own authority.

It grants no tools and no authority over status selection or completion; backlog authority alone owns selecting and claiming the recorded work.
