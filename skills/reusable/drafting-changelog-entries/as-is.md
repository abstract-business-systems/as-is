# Drafting Changelog Entries - as-is

## Purpose
Prepare concise history entries for the owning changelog procedure.

## Design
The skill summarizes the durable result, evidence, scope, and residual risk of a validated component task into a history entry, waiting for validated completion evidence and naming the task or change identity before summarizing checks and residual risk. It composes with sibling reusable skills that resolve the owning changelog and prepare scoped commits: it drafts the entry content but lets the owning changelog and commit procedures decide placement and cleanup. It holds no task authority, does not replace the owning changelog or completion protocol, and grants no tools; it establishes fit only.
