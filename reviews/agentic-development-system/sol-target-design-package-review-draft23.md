# Sol review of target-design package draft-23

This advisory, read-only review assessed the exact package snapshot at `drafts/agentic-development-system-target-design/` after the draft-22 findings. It does not authorize Grok invocation, user presentation, target adoption, task creation, or implementation.

## Verdict

**Revise — not ready for the Grok gate.** The package is substantially normalized and stronger than draft-22, but blocking inconsistencies remain in provenance, exact manifest schemas, scoring, diagram gates, source-artifact classification, and the Grok attempt record.

## Strengths

- The package consistently presents itself primarily as `target-design-v1-draft-23`, with a nine-file frozen set and eight non-manifest digest entries.
- Current `as-is.md` records are separated from live agent/skill artifacts and contract files in the narrative inventory.
- The ledger has 24 proposal reusable headings plus four additions, and 12 proposal master headings plus two additions.
- Design facilitation is correctly workflow-only through `target-workflow/design-facilitation-v1`.
- Setup ownership is materially improved through one authoritative resource/action matrix.
- The comparison design includes fail-closed intent, protected-input and permitted-difference concepts, explicit unavailable observations, terminal states, invalidation records, both sides, six cases, six dimensions, and a 72-cell denominator.
- Cost and time are not silently treated as zero.
- Prior budget-stopped Grok evidence is preserved as incomplete rather than treated as approval.

## Blocking issues

1. The package cites `reviews/agentic-development-system/sol-target-design-package-review-draft23.md`, which did not exist in the reviewed snapshot and pre-states the current review outcome.
2. The record inventory mixes `as-is.md` records with live source artifacts and contract files, and the ledger does not provide explicit treatment for every separately classified source. The distinction between seven agent source contracts, six production target-agent IDs, `thinking-companion` as a workflow holder, and the capability-probe fixture must be made auditable.
3. Nested comparison-manifest schemas remain partly prose-level. Exact types, cardinalities, enums, unavailable observations, protected-input/source mapping, and permitted-difference registry equality must be closed.
4. Cost/time handling is inconsistent: there is no run-level elapsed-time schema, reviewer-time unavailable reasons are not structurally represented, optional cost observation conflicts with required availability predicates, and an unavailable dimension is not a declared cell status or advancement outcome.
5. Invalidation ordering relies on a timestamp absent from the record schema and uses an implicit terminal-record exception; validity and admissibility need one explicit algorithm.
6. Baseline/candidate revisions are described both as differences and as matched protected inputs; treatment identity must be separated from matched controls and permitted workflow differences.
7. Scoring lacks complete repeated case/side aggregation, explicit unavailable-dimension handling, and a review-event schema matching its burden formula.
8. The diagram does not show all hard fail-closed admission branches or validator/reviewer/integration failure paths.
9. The Grok record needs a complete prior-attempt ledger, exact current-attempt result schema, one durable human-outcome location, and reconciliation of the handoff’s reported USD 0.133 observation.

## Required revisions

- Create the next package revision only after removing the circular/nonexistent current-review reference.
- Keep the primary affected-record table record-only and classify live sources/contracts separately, with explicit inventory-only treatment where no target mapping exists.
- Close all nested schemas and registries; define the run-level observations, invalidation record, validity predicate, treatment identity, and admissibility algorithm.
- Define predicate-to-cell-to-case-to-dimension-to-run-to-repeated aggregation and unavailable handling completely.
- Complete diagram gates and blocked paths.
- Normalize the Grok gate and prior-attempt evidence.
- Recalculate and verify all manifest digests after every final edit.

## Residual uncertainty

SHA-256 equality and trackedness were not independently confirmed through the review surface. Human holders, feature, seed, baseline/candidate revisions, final rubric approval, Grok provenance/trial, host enforcement, and all implementation remain unresolved. This verdict is advisory only.
